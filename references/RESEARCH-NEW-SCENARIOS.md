# Research: three new scenarios (subway, airport arrival, emergency)

Researched on 25 Nov 2026 for the ITB x CAU build. Every source below was read on 25 Nov 2026; the date after a source is the date the page itself carries.

How to read the labels:
- **[Official]** a government, operator or hospital page. **[Press]** a news report. **[Secondary]** a wiki, blog or travel guide.
- **UNVERIFIED** means it could not be confirmed. Do not ship it as fact; a Korean teammate checks it first.
- **Documented line** means the Korean wording comes from a named source. **Draft line** means it was written for this report in standard polite Korean. It is not a quote from real staff, and like every procedure file it stays `verified: false` until a Korean teammate checks it.

### Facts that changed in 2025–26 (check before any slide or script)
- **Foreign cards at subway kiosks:** since 17 Mar 2026 the 440 new kiosks in 273 Line 1–8 stations take Visa and Mastercard, with a fee of about 3.7%. They only buy single-journey tickets and tourist passes, though: a prepaid transport card is still charged in **cash** (at a kiosk or a convenience store).
- **e-Arrival Card:** foreigners who have not yet registered (which includes a newly arriving D-4 student) submit it online, from 3 days before arrival until immigration. K-ETA holders and registered foreigners are exempt.
- **Passes:** the 기후동행카드 30-day pass ended (last charge 31 Aug 2026). Its successor, 기후동행패스 (linked to the national 모두의카드), is still settling. The game should stay with a plain prepaid 교통카드.
- **Health insurance timing:** D-4 language students join national health insurance only **after 6 months** in Korea (D-2 degree students join from arrival). This changes what an ER visit costs in the first week, and it may make the existing clinic part inaccurate; see 3.7.

### Biggest build risks
1. **The subway needs the most new mechanics:** card tap at the gates, a kiosk screen, a train arrival and ride, and wayfinding by signs. None of this exists yet.
2. **No official source gives what an immigration officer or a ticket clerk says.** Their lines are drafts built on documented requirements and need a Korean teammate.
3. **The medical boundary:** the 119 dispatcher gives first-aid instructions in real life, and an ER visit invites diagnosis. Both parts must stop at reporting and registration.
4. **Rule conflicts:** "a level always starts outside" doesn't fit an airport arrival, or a bag left on a train (see 4.5).

## 1. Seoul subway

### 1.1 What a new student has to get right (real order)
1. **Get a transport card (교통카드).**
   - Buy a prepaid card at a convenience store (편의점) or a station kiosk, then charge it. Prepaid cards are charged **in cash**, in units of 1,000 or 10,000 won, at station kiosks or convenience stores. [Official: Seoul Q&A, 2026-10-06]
   - Or buy a **single-journey card (1회용 교통카드)** at a station machine for one trip: fare + 100 won + a 500 won deposit. [Official: Seoul Metro]
2. Go down an entrance (출입구) to the concourse (대합실).
3. **Tap in** on the reader **on the right side of the gate** (개찰구). [Official: Seoul Metro ticket guide: "개찰구 오른쪽 교통카드 단말기에 … 접촉하세요"]
4. **Pick the platform by the direction sign (방면).** Platform signs show the final destination and the next stop. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2024-01-31]
5. Wait behind the platform doors (승강장 안전문), board, and listen for your stop: "이번 역은 ○○역입니다. 내리실 문은 오른쪽입니다." [Press: Kyunghyang, 2025-10-03]
6. **Transfer (환승)** if needed, by following the line-colour signs.
7. **Tap out** at the destination. If the balance is too low, the gate stays shut ("삑- 잔액이 부족합니다"). Pay the difference at the **교통카드 정산·충전기 inside the gates**; there is no need to call staff. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2014, 2016/2020]
8. Leave by the right **exit number (출구)**.
9. **Entered on the wrong side?** Tap out and re-enter at the **same station (same line) within 15 minutes**. The base fare is not charged again; it counts as one transfer. This works only with prepaid or postpaid cards (not single-journey or commuter tickets), once per trip, and only on the Seoul-managed sections (all of Lines 2, 5, 8 and 9; parts of 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7). [Official: Seoul, 2023-06-30 and 2023-09-26]
10. **Single-journey card:** after exiting, put it in the **보증금 환급기** to get the 500 won back. A lost or damaged card forfeits the deposit. [Official: Seoul Metro; Seoul transport page, rev. 2025-06-12]

### 1.2 Fares [Official: Seoul Metro 운임안내]
| | Transport card | Single-journey card |
|---|---|---|
| Adult | **1,550 won** for the first 10 km | card fare + 100 won (**1,650 won**) |
| Youth | 900 won | adult single-journey fare |
| Child (6–12) | 550 won | same as the card fare |

- Beyond 10 km: +100 won per 5 km up to 50 km, then +100 won per 8 km (adult).
- **Youth means ages 13–18**, or 19–24 only for students "초·중·고등학교 등에 재학 중인" (enrolled at elementary, middle and high schools and the like). A university language course is not on that list, so a 20-year-old language student pays the **adult** fare. This makes a good honest question for the player: "학생 할인 돼요?"
- Early-morning discount: 20% off the base fare for the first ride before 06:30, with a prepaid or postpaid card.
- Bus ↔ subway transfers count if you board within 30 minutes of tapping out (60 minutes from 21:00 to 07:00), and only if you tapped out. [Official: Seoul fares page, rev. 2025-07-11]
- A fare rise is being discussed (Seoul Metro argued for a much higher fare in June 2026), but none was approved as of the sources read. [Press: Asia Economy, 2026-06-12]

**Can a foreigner use the cash-only machines?** Yes. They take Korean won notes and coins like anyone else's. The real obstacle for a new student is having no won, or only a foreign card.

### 1.4 Gates and what they say
- One beep for a normal card; two beeps for senior and disability passes (plus a yellow or red light). [Press: YTN, 2023-10-19]
- Low balance when exiting: "삑- 잔액이 부족합니다" [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2014-08-20], error code **E-14**, and the gate does not open [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2012-06-15].
- **Gate type:** a 2007 conference paper records **tripod turnstiles (삼발이식 회전문)** on Lines 1–4 and flap gates on Lines 5–8, citing a 2001 design document. Seoul Metro has been replacing the last manual wheelchair gates on Lines 1–8 with flap-type automatic gates (completion targeted for July 2026). The gate type at any given station today is **UNVERIFIED**: check photos.
- An **emergency gate (비상게이트)** with a call button sits at one end of the gate line. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2018]
- Coming but not needed for the game: card readers with a built-in screen (contract planned for late 2026), and about 600 "tagless" Bluetooth gates on Lines 1–8 with full use expected from January 2027. [Press: Wikitree, 2026-03-19 and 2026-09-25]

### 1.6 Reading the system: directions, numbers, exits, announcements
- **Direction (방면):** the platform sign shows the final destination and the next stop. Most lines run on the right; Line 1 towards Incheon and Suwon, the Suin–Bundang line and the Shinbundang line run on the left. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2024-01-31]
- **Line 2 is a loop:** 내선순환 runs **clockwise** and 외선순환 **anticlockwise**. "시청에서 왕십리 방면이 내선순환, 시청에서 신촌 방면이 외선순환이다." [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2017-04-11]
- **Station numbers:** Line 2 is numbered clockwise from 시청 (201); 을지로입구 is 202 [Press: Money Today, 2013]. On Line 4, 명동 is 424 and 동작 is 431 [Secondary]. On Line 9, 흑석 (919) and 동작 (920) are **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Station entrances:** the entrance pillar (출입구 안내기둥) is standardised as **dark grey with white text**, and an entrance at a transfer station lists every line. [Official: Seoul signage guideline, 2013]
- **Exit signs:** yellow is the commonly described exit colour. **UNVERIFIED** (only a forum post).
- **Platform doors:** station names are on large and small stickers on the moving doors and emergency doors, with bigger letters on a brighter background since 2023. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2023-04-21]
- **Announcements:**
  - Documented line: "이번 역은 ○○역입니다. 내리실 문은 오른쪽입니다." ("This stop is ○○. The doors open on the right.") The voice is the voice actor 강희선, who has recorded it for 29 years. [Press: Kyunghyang, 2025-10-03]
  - Documented line (platform doors): "안전문이 열립니다(닫힙니다)." ("The safety doors are opening (closing).") 안전문 replaced 스크린도어 in 2013. [Official: Seoul, 2013]
  - The in-car displays used the fragments "이번역은", "○○행으로 가실 고객께서는" (the start of the transfer notice) and "This Stop is". [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2023-05-17]
  - The full transfer sentence and the English wording are **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Transfers:** big interchanges such as 왕십리 use floor arrows coloured by line. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2024]

### 1.7 Station staff and lost property
- **Where staff are:** a help booth beside the gates (described as a round glass booth), and the 고객안내센터 (station office) in the concourse. The booth can be empty while staff patrol. Help is also on the call buttons of the ticket, refund and fare-adjustment machines, on the emergency gate, on 4–6 SOS phones per platform, and on the intercom at each end of every car. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2018-10-22]
- **Name of the office:** current Seoul Metro texts call the station office **고객안전실**. [Official: Seoul Metro lost-property page; Seoul, 2025-06] The older word 역무실 still appears in articles. Put 고객안전실 on the sign and teach 역무실 in the key words.
- **Seoul Metro call centre: 1577-1234.** [Official] Korail: 1544-7788. (A Seoul Metro cartoon shows 1544-1234, which looks like an error. **UNVERIFIED**.)
- **What to tell staff, the official mnemonic "아!차!내!짐!":** 아! "물건을 두고 내렸을 땐" (when you've left something behind) → 차량 시간 (when the train was), 내린 칸 위치 (which car you got off), 짐의 위치 (where your bag was). Contact the nearest 고객안전실 or 1577-1234 at once. [Official: Seoul, 2025-06]
- **Car and door numbers:** numbers from **1-1 to 10-4** (car–door, Lines 1–4) are painted on the platform. "대충 앞쪽이에요. 중간쯤이에요." ("Roughly near the front. Somewhere in the middle.") is the kind of vague answer that makes the search hard. Staff may **check your transport card to find when you got off**, then identify the train, check whether it is still in service, and ask stations ahead to search it. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2018-11-30]
- **After that:** the station registers the item online (the Seoul Metro page now links to **경찰민원24**, the police portal that replaced lost112; the exact switch date is **UNVERIFIED**). It goes to the line's lost-property centre, which processes it within 7 days, then to the police, who keep it **6 months** (a 2022 article said 9), then to the state. You collect it in person with ID. [Official: Seoul Metro lost-property page]

### 1.8 Physical details for the 3D artist
Sizes marked "est." are estimates for blocking out only. Check them against photos.

- **Concourse (대합실):** a long hall under the street with the gate line across it. In the unpaid area: ticket machines and kiosks in a row against a wall, the help booth beside the gates, the 고객안전실 door and window nearby, often a convenience store, and the deposit refund machines on the exit side. In the paid area: the 정산·충전기, then stairs and escalators down, each marked with its direction (○○ 방면). Est. 40 × 20 m section, ceiling about 3 m.
- **Gate line:** 6–10 gates plus a wide accessible gate and the emergency gate. Each gate cabinet has the card reader on top at the **right-hand** side of the lane. Older Line 1–4 stations have tripod arms; accessible lanes have flaps. Est. cabinet 1.6–1.8 m long and about 1 m high; est. lane width 0.55–0.6 m, wide lane about 0.9 m (**UNVERIFIED**: no official dimension found).
- **Machines:** free-standing kiosks with a touch screen, a note slot and a coin slot (a card slot on the new ones), a call button, and the T-money logo on older units. Est. 0.9 W × 0.7 D × 1.8 H m.
- **Platform and doors:** underground platforms have **floor-to-ceiling platform doors (완전 밀폐형)**; above-ground stations have railing-type doors about 1.5 m high. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2004] Station-name stickers sit on the doors, and the car–door number (e.g. 4-2) on the platform. A Line 2 train has 10 cars, so a real platform is roughly 200 m long (derived: 10 × 19.5 m). Build a 2–3 car section.
- **Car interior:**
  - **Size:** 19.5 m long, about 3.16 m wide, 4 doors per side [Secondary: Wikipedia, uncited]. Door width **1,300 mm**, the standard spec dating from Line 1 [Academic: Journal of the Korean Society for Railway, 2014].
  - **Seats:** long side benches. The 7 seats in the middle are 교통약자 배려석, and the 2 end seats of that block are the 임산부 배려석 (pregnant women's seats). The ends of the car hold the priority seats (교통약자 지정석): 12 per car, 19 in all. [Official: Seoul, 2016-01-08] The pink colour of the pregnant women's seats comes from a Seoul mediahub headline seen in search results ("분홍색 '임산부 배려석' 지하철 1~8호선 확대"); the article itself was not opened, so treat the shade as **UNVERIFIED** and check photos.
  - **Fittings:** a passenger information display above each door showing the arriving station, the door side and notices [Official: Seoul], an emergency intercom at each car end [Official: 2018], plus straps, poles and luggage racks (선반).
- **고객안전실 window:** a counter window in the concourse wall with glass and a speaking gap. Behind it sit CCTV monitors and desks. Est. counter 1.0–1.1 m high. The exact look is **UNVERIFIED**: check photos.
- **Signage:** dark grey entrance pillars with white text; the line number in its colour; overhead direction boards that use colour only for lines (2013 guideline); yellow exit signs (**UNVERIFIED**).

### 1.10 Recommended parts

#### Part 1: Get a transport card and ride home
**Story idea:** classes are over and you're in Myeongdong. Get a transport card and take the subway back to your dormitory near 흑석역. **Route:** 명동 (Line 4, towards 사당) → 동작 → change to Line 9 → 흑석. A smaller build is one ride without the transfer. Whether the 4 ↔ 9 transfer at 동작 has its own transfer gate is **UNVERIFIED**, and so is the Line 9 direction from 동작 to 흑석.

**Steps:**
1. Street → station entrance (dark grey pillar).
2. Convenience store in the concourse, conversation below. This step grants `doc:tcard`.
3. Tap in on the right-hand reader. Mechanic: a new tap interactable.
4. Choose the stairs by the 방면 sign. A wrong choice puts you on the opposite platform; the lesson is the 15-minute re-entry rule.
5. Train arrives; the doors open ("안전문이 열립니다"); ride; the announcement plays.
6. (Stretch) transfer at 동작.
7. Tap out, and find the exit.

#### Part 2: Left my bag on the train
**Story idea:** you get off at 명동 and the doors close. Your backpack is still on the luggage rack. Report it before the train gets too far.
- **Start:** on the platform as the train leaves. This needs a rule exception (see 4.5).
- **First to-do:** "Note where you got off". The player interacts with the car–door number on the platform and the clock, which grants `seen:door` (a flag). A choice with `needs: seen:door` shows the exact answer; without the flag only the vague answers remain.

### 1.11 Learner mistakes to draw wrong answers from (subway)
- **Confused words:**
  - 충전 (top up) / 환전 (exchange money) / 환불 (refund)
  - 잃어버리다 (lose) / 잊어버리다 (forget)
  - 출구 (exit) / 입구 (entrance) / 승강장 (platform)
  - 방면 (direction) read as the station you are at
  - 내선 / 외선 on Line 2
  - 몇 호선 (which line) / 몇 번 출구 (which exit)
  - 타다 (get on) / 내리다 (get off) / 갈아타다 (transfer)
- **Wrong register:** 반말 to the clerk or to station staff.
- **Procedural misunderstandings:**
  - charging a card with a foreign credit card;
  - calling staff for a low balance instead of using the 정산·충전기;
  - leaving through the gate to change platforms without knowing the 15-minute rule;
  - expecting a student discount at 20;
  - throwing the single-journey card away without taking back the deposit.
- **Vague answers:** "아까" (a while ago) and "대충 앞쪽" (roughly the front), where the exact time and the car–door number are needed.

## 2. Arriving at Incheon International Airport

### 2.1 The arrival flow (real order)
1. **Before landing:** submit the **e-Arrival Card** online (free), any time from 3 days before arrival until immigration. It is void if you don't enter within 72 hours. It is required for short-stay foreigners and for long-stay foreigners **who have not yet registered**; exempt are K-ETA holders, registered foreigners, group-visa holders and crew. [Official: MOIS national Q&A 110, 2026-01-15]
   - It became mandatory for all foreign nationals from 1 Jan 2026 [Press: EY tax alert], and paper cards were still accepted in parallel in March 2026 [Secondary: iVisa]. The paper-card status today is **UNVERIFIED**.
   - Note: the Study in Korea page says stays over 90 days don't need an arrival card, which conflicts with the 2026 Q&A. Follow the newer Q&A.
2. **Quarantine:** only travellers who visited a 중점검역관리지역 must submit Q-CODE (a health questionnaire). Vietnamese regions have appeared on the KDCA lists, which change every quarter (**UNVERIFIED** for any given date). [Official: KDCA]
3. **Immigration (입국심사):**
   - Koreans queue on the **blue line** and foreigners on the **red line**. Foreigners show their passport and arrival card. Foreigners aged 17 and over give fingerprints and a face image. [Official: Incheon Airport, 유인입국심사]
   - Priority lanes are for diplomats, crew, people with walking difficulties, children under 7, people aged 70+, pregnant women, Korea Priority Card holders and recommended business people. [Official: Incheon Airport]
   - **Order at the booth:** passport and arrival card → **both index fingers** scanned → face photo → inspection. Registered foreigners give all ten fingers (at registration). The system is FBIS, in use since January 2012. [Official: MOJ]
   - Foreigners receive an entry stamp. [Official: Korea Immigration Service]
   - First-time students use a staffed booth. The automated gates (SES) are for registered foreigners aged 17+ without pre-registration, and for short-stay foreigners only at departure. [Official: Incheon Airport; MOJ, 2024-09-06]
4. **What the officer checks**, by law. Immigration Act, Article 12(3): the passport and visa are valid, "**the purpose of his or her entry shall comply with the status of stay**", the period of stay is set, and the person is not banned. Under 12(4), entry can be refused if the person **can't show this**. Under Article 12-2, refusing fingerprints or a photo can lead to refused entry. [Official: KLRI English translation]
   - **No official list of the questions officers ask was found.** Purpose, school, address and length of stay follow from the checks above. Many students report few or no questions; **UNVERIFIED**.
5. **Baggage claim:** find your flight's carousel number on the display. Baggage claim and the oversized-baggage area are on the 1st floor. Allow 1–2 hours from landing before booking onward transport. [Official: Study in Korea]
6. **Customs (세관):**
   - Two lanes, "**신고있음**" (something to declare) and "**신고없음**" (nothing to declare). Customs may still inspect people in the 신고없음 lane at random. [Official: Incheon Airport, 세관신고]
   - Since 1 May 2023 a traveller with nothing to declare fills in no form. Declarations can be made in the '여행자 세관신고' app or on the web. [Official: Incheon Airport; KCS via korea.kr]
   - **Duty-free:** USD 800 per person; 2 bottles of alcohol (2 L, USD 400 in total); 200 cigarettes; 100 ml of perfume.
   - **Must declare:** cash and similar over USD 10,000, and **animals, meat, dairy, plants and fruit** (quarantine).
7. **Quarantine on food:** the 2026 summer campaign targeted the **China, Vietnam, Thailand and Mongolia routes**, with detector dogs and fines of **up to 10 million won** for illegally brought farm products; fruit and sausages were named. [Official: MAFRA, 2026-07-23]
8. **Arrivals hall (입국장):** exchange counters, telecom counters, a convenience store (transport card), the information desk, bus ticket counters, and the airport railroad in the transport centre (교통센터) one floor down.

### 3.2 Calling 119
**The official reporting order**, with example lines (Documented lines; a children's safety page of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety that credits the National Fire Agency):
1. Say there's a patient first. "119입니다. 무슨일이세요?" ("119. What's happening?") → "환자가 있습니다!" ("There's a patient!")
2. Give the exact address. "환자가 있는 주소를 말씀해 주세요." ("Tell me the address where the patient is.") If you don't know it: turn on the phone's GPS and give landmarks, such as a large building's name and phone number, the lift's number, or the utility-pole number.
3. Describe the patient. "환자가 의식이 있고, 숨은 쉬고 있나요? 어느 부위가 아픈가요?" ("Is the patient conscious, and breathing? Where does it hurt?")
4. Give the patient's age and any conditions. "환자의 나이와 평소에 지병이 있으면 말씀해 주세요."
5. Give your name and another number. "전화하신 분 이름과 통화가능한 다른 연락처를 알려주세요."
6. Stay on the line. "전화를 끊지 말고 의료지도를 받고 … 구급차를 기다립니다." ("Don't hang up; follow the medical guidance and wait for the ambulance.") **The same page continues with CPR instructions. The game must not show those** (see 3.6).

**Other ways to report:**
- Text (SMS/MMS) with a photo or video, the 119 신고 app (sends GPS) and video calls.
- For "외국인과 같이 음성으로 의사소통할 수 없는 경우" (foreigners who can't communicate by voice), attaching a photo that shows the address connects faster. [Official: korea.kr, 2024-06-05]

**Interpretation:**
- Seoul's 119 runs a **건강콜센터** inside the Seoul disaster centre that interprets for foreign callers (since 2013). It interpreted 2,429 calls in 2013: English 67.6%, Chinese 17.7%, Japanese 11.4%. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2014]
- Other provinces use **three-way calls** with volunteer interpreters (Jeonbuk: 16 languages including Vietnamese). [Press: Asia Economy, 2025-10-31]
- **Vietnamese and Indonesian interpretation on Seoul 119 today is UNVERIFIED.**
- The police line 112 has its own foreign-language centre (English and Chinese, other languages through contracted interpreters; 21,252 foreign-language calls in 2025; an AI interpreter in 5 languages including Vietnamese is planned). [Press: 2026]

**Physical setting:** the existing 명동길 Hub at dusk, a man on the ground in front of a shop, a few bystanders, then a 119 ambulance with paramedics. The ambulance livery is **UNVERIFIED**: check photos.

### 3.3 Hospital ER at night (응급실)
- **Real order, as two hospitals publish it:**
  - Asan Medical Center: fill in a **진료신청서** at the ER desk (응급수납) and get a 진료접수증 → triage (분류실) assigns a treatment area by severity → the nurse there takes the slip, the doctor examines → the doctor decides discharge or admission → pay at the ER desk and get a receipt (and an outpatient appointment slip) → show the receipt to the nurse, get the medicine or a prescription for an outside pharmacy.
  - Severance does triage (예진실) first, then registration with the 원무팀; zones are 중환 (severe), 경환 (mild) and 소아 (children).
  - Treatment goes **by severity, not arrival order**. [Official: AMC; Severance; Hanyang University Hospital]
- **Triage:** Korea uses KTAS, five levels (소생, 긴급, 응급, 준응급, 비응급) [Press, 2024-09]. The hospital pages say only "분류실" or "예진실"; that a nurse usually does it is common practice but **UNVERIFIED** here.
- **Costs:**
  - From **13 Sep 2024**, patients triaged **KTAS 4–5** (mild or non-urgent) at large emergency centres pay **90%** of the bill themselves (50–60% before); at 지역응급의료센터 this applies to KTAS 5 only. The average share went from about 130,000 to about 220,000 won. [Press: Newsis, 2024-09-11; the rule is in the National Health Insurance Act enforcement rule]
  - Whether this still applies in late 2026 is **UNVERIFIED**.
  - AMC adds that non-emergency patients without a referral pay the full insured rate (100%).
- **ID:** since 20 May 2024, hospitals check ID when you use national insurance (passport or ARC accepted). Emergency patients and under-19s were **reported** as exceptions [Press, 2024]; the official text was not opened, so treat the exception as **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Insurance timing for our persona:** see 3.7. A first-week D-4 student is **not yet in national health insurance**.

### 3.4 Public night pharmacy (공공심야약국)
- Seoul runs **39** public night pharmacies in all 25 districts, open **22:00–01:00**; 28 are open every day of the year. In 2025 they made 249,029 sales: 79.5% non-prescription medicine, 11.0% prescriptions.
- Find them on the Seoul city site, Naver, the 손목닥터9988 app or the Smart Seoul Map. [Official: Seoul mediahub, 2026-01-29]
- About 240 operate nationwide (July 2026) [Press: Dailypharm]; the national portal is e-gen.or.kr.
- **Why not now:** a playable visit centres on the pharmacist recommending medicine, which is the content the game must not give.

### 3.5 Police box: lost wallet or phone
- Report a loss at the nearest 지구대 or 파출소, or online. Found items are registered on the police lost-property system. [Official: korea.kr; Seoul Metro]

- **A lost residence card (ARC)** can be reported online on HiKorea [Official: MOJ notice]. You apply for a new one at immigration, reportedly within **14 days**, for a fee of 30,000–35,000 won (sources conflict). [Secondary, **UNVERIFIED**]

### 4.4 New mechanics
1. **Card tap at a gate:**
   - a reader on the right-hand side;
   - the gate opens if the card is valid and has money, with one beep and an animation;
   - otherwise it stays shut with "잔액이 부족합니다";
   - the 정산·충전기 inside the gate clears the shortfall.
   - **Needs a money balance**, a new numeric state (the game tracks only flags now). The smallest version is flags: `doc:tcard` + `doc:tcard_charged`.
2. **Kiosk and machine screens:** language choice, buy a single-journey card (destination), charge, insert cash; the deposit refund machine. Build them from the UI kit as a screen card; keep them to 3–4 buttons.
3. **Train arrival and ride:**
   - arrival on the platform: doors open with "안전문이 열립니다" and close;
   - the ride: a car interior with motion faked by tunnel lights passing the windows, the announcement and the door-side display;
   - arrival: doors open at the destination.
   - Use fades between stations; one stop is enough.
4. **Wayfinding by signs:** choosing stairs and platforms by 방면, finding the exit number, the bus stop number. A wrong choice gets a realistic consequence (the wrong platform, then the 15-minute re-entry lesson) rather than a fail.
5. **Airport handling:** the serpentine queue, picking your own suitcase off the carousel (identify it by tag or colour), choosing a customs lane by walking through it, and the face-camera action (aim the view at the camera).
6. **Phone call:**
   - a speaker with no 3D body (the name strip reads "119 상황실");
   - the voice played through a phone filter, a new audio effect;
   - an "on the line" state that ends when the ambulance arrives.
   - The answers stay multiple choice; **no typing**.
7. **Location answers from the world:** the "where are you" choices are built from shop and building names visible near the player (data-driven from the Hub's fictional signs). The right answer is the one the player can actually see.
8. **Ambulance arrival:** a vehicle with lights and a siren, and paramedics walking to the patient.
