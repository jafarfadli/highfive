# Pitch research: the language barrier for international students in Korea

Research for the Global Startup Competition 2026 deck (Vietnam, 8–11 Nov 2026), collected on 19 Oct 2026.

Official sources come first. "Derived" means our own arithmetic on official numbers, and "unverified" means a figure we could not confirm and must not put on a slide. Every number on a slide needs its source in the speaker notes or the appendix.

Two different counts are both called "international students", so label which one a slide uses:
- **MOE/KEDI 교육기본통계** counts foreign students enrolled at higher-education institutions on 1 April.
- **MOJ 통계월보** counts holders of D-2, D-4-1 and D-4-7 visas each month, including people who have since fallen into illegal stay.

### Why universities pay: IEQAS (교육국제화역량 인증제)
- **Language programmes**, to be certified: illegal stay under 8–12%, NHIS enrolment of at least 95%, completion of at least 50%, and Korean-law education for at least 60% of students.
- **Degree programmes**, to be certified: illegal stay under 1–2% and dropout under 6–8%.
- **What failing costs:** universities rated "비자정밀 심사" are in principle refused visas for a year. From the second half of 2026 that applies to 16 degree and 4 language programmes, and sanctions can now last up to 3 years.
- **What passing earns:** simpler visa checks and priority for GKS scholarships.

Source for all of the above: MOE, 12 Feb 2026: https://www.moe.go.kr/boardCnts/viewRenew.do?boardID=294&boardSeq=105347

- **D-4-1 overstayers:** 29.5% in Feb 2026 (22,158 of 75,033), down from 53.8% in 2021 (MOJ via Maeil, 15 Apr 2026: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026041511275422136).
- **Support works:** Hansung University's multilingual one-stop support cut its illegal-stay rate from 6.86% (2022) to 0.58% (2025).
- **Policy signal:** the MOE 「외국인유학생 인재양성 혁신전략」 (8 Sep 2026) plans 24-hour AI chatbot counselling in students' native languages. From 2029 it will grade support programmes on content and quality, and it raises the required Korean-ability share from 40% to 60% by 2030. https://www.niied.go.kr/web/main/nid/niied_media/5842
- **Positioning:** the government sees the problem but is building helplines. Our place is the **rehearsal before the visit**, a layer that helplines and chatbots don't cover.

No product we found rehearses Korean counter procedures with their document dependencies: the ARC at immigration, a bank account that needs the ARC, or a clinic visit from reception to doctor to payment. The existing tools fall into three groups:

| Group | Products | What they don't do |
|---|---|---|

| **Adaptation platforms and quizzes** | **Koready** (O!ng Lab, Korea): "supports adaption and long-term life planning in Korea", Koready Skills courses, "Trusted by 10,000+ learners worldwide", and the label **"Be Koready"** (verified in its site bundle, 19 Oct 2026: https://www.ko-ready.com). **nunchi.kr** (DULGI COMPANY, 2026): a free 3-minute "Nunchi Test Game" with 5 social scenarios and no admin procedures (https://nunchi.kr/home.html). **Rehearse: Korean Practice**, a scenario-based speaking app (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rehearse-korean-practice/id6670446571). | Rehearse the visit itself: its order, its documents, and the counter's own Korean |

- **Partner, not rival:** HireVisa does the paperwork, and we rehearse the visits that remain. Universities already sign with student-facing startups.

## 4. Business-model evidence

### Who pays (ranked by how realistic it is for a two-person team)
1. **Universities' international offices and 어학당 (B2B).**
   - They have a reason to pay: IEQAS.
     - Language programmes must reach NHIS enrolment of at least 95%, Korean-law education for at least 60% of students, and illegal stay under 8–12%.
     - Failing means a one-year visa ban: 20 programmes are banned from the second half of 2026.
     - From 2029, support programmes will be graded on quality.
   - Proof that support works: Hansung's multilingual one-stop support cut illegal stay from 6.86% to 0.58%.
   - Universities already buy from student-facing startups (HireVisa) and host bank-funded education sessions.
2. **Banks sponsoring the bank scenario.**
   - The five major banks had 6.97M foreign customers in Mar 2026 and are adding products for them ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/business/banking-finance/20260420/banks-expand-services-for-foreign-customers-as-migrant-numbers-rise)).
   - Hana had 2.53M foreign customers in 2025.
   - Foreign workers sent $3.49bn home in 2024 ([The Fact](https://news.tf.co.kr/read/economy/2252761.htm)).
   - Banks already run financial-education sessions for international students: Hana with the Seoul Global Center at Korea, Dongguk and Yonsei; Toss Bank with NIIED; NH with the Vietnamese Student Association.
   - **No public sponsorship rate exists, so this is a hypothesis to test with a letter of intent (LOI).**
3. **Pre-departure channels (B2B2C).**
   - Vietnam: 85,896 TOPIK applicants and 22 King Sejong Institutes.
   - Indonesia: 10 King Sejong Institutes.
   - Study-abroad agencies: 17.8% of students chose their university mainly on an agency's advice (NRF 2026 survey via MOE).
4. **B2G project contracts.**
   - MOJ early-adaptation programme: 37,514 participants in 2025.
   - KIIP: 384 institutions and 90,180 participants in 2025 ([MOJ](https://www.immigration.go.kr/moj/2418/subview.do)).
   - The only price reference is a ₩40M TOPIK-content tender (Daeduk, Aug 2025). This needs a registered company, so it is medium term.
5. **B2C freemium, the weakest stream.**
   - Median download-to-paid is 2.1% worldwide but **0.7% in India and Southeast Asia**, where revenue per install is $0.11 at day 60 ([RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/report)). So 10,000 installs ≈ $1,100.
   - Use B2C for reach and data, not for revenue.

### Licences that affect money
- **OmniVoice weights are CC-BY-NC** ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/k2-fsa/OmniVoice)). They are fine for the competition demo. Anything paid needs a re-bake with a commercial TTS:
  - Azure is about $1.50 per 100k characters, so a full re-bake costs under $50.
  - CLOVA Voice forbids storing audio files, so it can't be used to pre-bake.
- **Music was generated with Suno.** Only songs made on a paid plan may be used commercially. Record which plan was used, or re-make the tracks.
- **The crowd ambience has no recorded source or licence.** Find it or replace it.
- **OSM (ODbL) allows commercial games.** Credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" legibly, and be ready to share the cleaned building data on request ([OSMF FAQ](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Licence_and_Legal_FAQ)).

### Assumptions that are ours, not data (label them so on slides)
- The per-student price.
- The share of universities that will buy.
- Sponsorship fees.
- Conversion above the 0.7–2.1% benchmarks.
- Learning outcomes. There is no user-test data yet; the only telemetry is from the team's own play-tests.

## 5. Getting it onto laptops and phones (Unity 6 Web)

Researched 19 Oct 2026; nothing was built yet.

- **Desktop browsers: feasible now.** Unity 6 Web (WebGL 2) runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on laptops. A hosted build is about **4–7 person-days** including a measuring spike, and it gives judges and universities a no-install link.
  - Brotli compression needs HTTPS.
  - Audio starts only after the first click.
  - No runtime TTS on the web, and never put an API key in a web build.
  - Telemetry must be sent to an endpoint, because the web's `persistentDataPath` is the player's own IndexedDB.
- **Phones: a separate "Web Lite" build, about 20–35 person-days.** A clinic-only slice for Android Chrome is the realistic November scope.
  - Unity 6 supports iOS Safari 15+ and Android Chrome ([Unity 6.4 docs](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.4/Documentation/Manual/webgl-browsercompatibility.html)), but Unity engineers advise keeping Safari under **300–500 MB** of memory.
  - An empty URP web build already uses about 219 MB, and our content is roughly 200 MB+ uncompressed.
  - CrazyGames turns Unity games off on iOS by default because of memory crashes ([requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)).
- **Traps specific to this repo:**
  - Voices are Decompress On Load, so iPhones in Silent Mode would mute them ([Unity audio docs](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.4/Documentation/Manual/webgl-audio.html)).
  - Stitching PA pieces with `GetData` only works with that load type; switch to `PlayScheduled`.
  - DXT textures are unpacked in software on phones (about 373 MB), so use ASTC.
  - The Ink pass forces a depth+normals prepass.
  - About 38 pedestrians are skinned on the CPU.
  - The Korean fonts are 12 MB (subset them).
  - Home and Select are full copies of the Hub.
  - Character and Hair use `nointerpolation`, which hit an iOS 18 "context lost" bug (fixed in iOS 18.5).
  - There are no touch controls.
  - itch.io embeds games in an iframe, and Safari has no IndexedDB in iframes, so on iPhone, saves and caches may not persist. Host at the top level instead.
- **Native Android** is the more reliable phone path: real threads, GPU skinning, and an APK installed from a QR code.
  - Play Store needs 12 testers for 14 days on new personal accounts, too slow for November.
  - iOS TestFlight needs a paid account and review.
- **Plan for 8–11 Nov:**
  1. A desktop web build and a QR landing page.
  2. A gameplay video for phones.
  3. Optionally a lite Clinic part 1 slice.
  4. An Android APK as plan B.

## 7. Proposed business model (desktop-first)

Decided 19 Oct 2026: the prototype stays a desktop app (Windows and macOS). Desktop suits institutions, not individuals.
- Universities run orientation in classrooms and labs.
- Pre-departure centres teach in classrooms.
- The app works offline, because the voices are baked.
- There is no app-store cut.

The model is therefore **B2B2C**: institutions pay, and students use it free.
