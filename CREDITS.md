## Textures, shaders and models

- **Surface textures** (`Assets/_Project/Art/Generated/Textures/`: paving, facades, roofs, floors, ceiling tiles, wood, fabric, metal) and the **room atlases** seen through windows are painted procedurally by the project's own editor code (`TextureKit.cs`, `RoomAtlas.cs`); no third-party textures.
- **Street backdrops** behind the interior windows and on the results screen (`Backdrop_*.png`) are renders of the project's own hub scene (`SeoulSceneBuilder.CaptureBackdrops`).
- **Shaders** `SeoulFirstWeek/Surface` (cel-shaded triplanar), `SeoulFirstWeek/Interior Window` (interior mapping), `Character`, `Hair`, `Ink Outline` (full-screen ink lines) and `UI Ink` are original to the project (`Assets/_Project/Art/Shaders/`). The triplanar normal blend follows Ben Golus's published "whiteout" technique.
- **UI ink shapes and the print texture** (`InkShape.cs`, `Art/Generated/UI/InkNoise.png`) are generated by the project's own code (`InkKit.cs`); the art direction is inspired by the reference images in `references/art/` (not shipped).
- **People, props, furniture and signage** are built from Unity primitives by the scene builder (`HumanRig.cs`, `StreetProps.cs`, `InteriorKit.cs`, `FacadeDresser.cs`); no third-party models.

Takes are checked with OpenAI Whisper small (MIT).
